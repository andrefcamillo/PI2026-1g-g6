Photo Finish

Laser beams, electronic touch pads, and photo-finish cameras determine the fate of Olympic athletes, capturing data with millisecond precision and ensuring that their efforts are measured with impressive accuracy and efficiency.

False Start Sensors

Special starting blocks feature sensors capable of detecting the force exerted by the athlete during the start. If the athlete applies pressure to the block before the official signal, the system detects the movement and automatically records a false start. This technology enhances precision in competitions, reducing officiating errors and ensuring more reliable results.

Laser Measurement

It replaced the old tape measures. A laser beam precisely calculates the exact distance of jumps and throws.

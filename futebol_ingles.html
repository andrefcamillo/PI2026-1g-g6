Semi-automated offside

Semi-automated offside technology uses smart cameras and artificial intelligence to track the ball and players' bodies in real time. At the moment of the pass, the system calculates 3D virtual lines within seconds and sends an alert to the VAR booth, where human officials quickly and accurately confirm the play before notifying the on-field referee.

Tie-breaker

In soccer, the replay analysis tool is a relatively old technology, yet it has undergone significant evolution in recent years. It is a resource used by commentators to explain specific plays with greater precision. Through virtualization or digitization, it is possible to carefully analyze whether an offside occurred, whether the ball crossed the goal line, or the distance and speed of a shot. This tool serves a purely illustrative purpose, helping viewers and commentators better understand the game.

-

GPS vest

The GPS vest is a device used to monitor athlete performance during training and competitions. Using sensors, it records data such as distance covered, speed, acceleration, and positioning, aiding in performance analysis and training planning.

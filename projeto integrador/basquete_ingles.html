Dynamic LED Courts

A modern flooring surface that exceeds all technical requirements for traditional sports flooring. Customizable, touchscreen-controlled LED game lines allow for seamless transitions between various sports and activities. They enable instant changes to line markings, the display of real-time animations and game statistics, and the easy transformation of the space for other sports disciplines.

Airless Gen1

After six years of development, the Airless Gen1 utilized cutting-edge software to conceive, design, and create the ball. Everything was rigorously tested at W Labs by top engineers. The result: a basketball that defies convention by delivering exceptional performance, even without air.

CBB Radar

In basketball, one example is CBB Radar, an active platform that monitors—in near real-time—all Brazilian players competing in professional leagues in Brazil and abroad. The tool consolidates information from multiple international sources—such as USBasket, LatinBasket, the NCAA, and European leagues—into a single system. The platform is capable of generating specialized reports, such as an individual multidimensional assessment that considers efficiency, volume, shooting profile, defensive impact, and tactical context.

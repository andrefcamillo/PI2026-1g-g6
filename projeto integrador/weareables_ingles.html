Jump Trackers

These are small sensors (usually placed on the lower back) that act as accelerometers and gyroscopes. They quantify the height of each jump and the total volume of jumps during a workout. Their purpose is to monitor joint load.

-

Whoop

It is a recovery and physiological monitor currently used by elite athletes. These are screenless wristbands or elastic straps worn on the forearm, wrist, or chest. It measures heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), sleep quality, and rest time.

-

Smart Textiles

The use of clothing with integrated sensors has gained ground for more detailed body monitoring. They measure continuous biometrics, sweat rates, and biomechanical parameters during movement.
